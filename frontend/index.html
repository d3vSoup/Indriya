What Indriya Does

India has over 18 million deaf people and millions more with visual impairments. Most cannot access quality classroom education. Indriya changes that, today, with zero hardware cost.

sign_language

Real-Time Sign Language

When a teacher speaks, Indriya instantly translates every word into beautiful Indian Sign Language animations on the deaf student's screen — in proper ISL grammar. No interpreter needed.

braille

Virtual Braille Keyboard

Blind students type notes in Braille on any standard keyboard — no ₹45,000 Braille device needed. Every keypress gives instant audio feedback, and teacher broadcasts are read aloud automatically.

auto_detect_voice

Silent Handshake

Before a lesson starts, Indriya silently detects the student's accessibility needs and auto-configures itself. No pop-ups, no questions — just seamless, instant adaptation.

handshake The Silent Handshake

Before a lesson begins, the platform performs a sensory handshake. CSS media queries and behavioral cues auto-configure the environment. No intrusive questionnaires required.
